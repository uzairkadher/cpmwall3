CPM Wall

Privacy Policy | Effective Date: October 2026

A. Introduction and scope

CPM Wall operates a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform for web publishers, currently building infrastructure for future content-unlocking tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how CPM Wall ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information. It applies to publishers who register for our platform and visitors interacting with our tools on publisher websites.

B. Information we collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate, secure, and build our services:

  • Publisher Information: Email addresses, website URLs, and account configuration details provided during registration.
  • Technical and Usage Information: When users interact with our platform, we automatically log IP addresses, browser and device specifications, and security request metadata.

C. Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not require or intentionally collect sensitive personal or financial data, including:

  • Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) numbers or government IDs.
  • Bank account details or payment information.
  • Private personal communications.
  • Publisher account passwords in a readable (unhashed) format.

D. Purposes of processing

We process this information to:

  • Administer publisher accounts and deliver platform infrastructure.
  • Monitor system stability and performance.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and automated abuse.
  • Respond to lawful authority requests and legal obligations.

E. Cookies and future advertising integrations

We currently use strictly necessary technical cookies to maintain service security and manage active user sessions.

Future Ad Networks: We currently do not serve third-party advertisements. We plan to integrate third-party advertising networks in the future to enable our core advertising-supported content-unlocking features. This Privacy Policy will be updated to detail third-party tracking, advertising cookies, and consent mechanisms before any advertising network is deployed on the platform.

F. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We share information only with:

  • Infrastructure Partners: Secure cloud hosting and security providers necessary to operate the platform.
  • Legal Authorities: Competent regulators, courts, or law enforcement when legally compelled, or when necessary to protect our systems from serious security threats.

G. Data retention and security

We retain collected information only for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain active accounts, resolve disputes, secure our logs, and comply with legal obligations. We use industry-standard technical safeguards, such as encrypted transit and restricted access controls, to protect data.

H. User rights and deletion

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To request permanent deletion of your account data or to submit a privacy inquiry, email admin@cpmwall.tech. Note that certain security logs must be retained temporarily for network defense and legal compliance before being overwritten.

I. Pakistan cybercrime compliance

CPM Wall is based in Pakistan and strictly monitors infrastructure to prevent abuse. Under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016 (as amended), we maintain system logs to defend against unauthorized access (Section 3) and unauthorized copying or transmission of data (Section 4). We cooperate with competent authorities, including the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA), to investigate unlawful interference affecting our platform.

J. International users and GDPR

For data originating from the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, individuals maintain their rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR, including the right to data portability, the right to restrict processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a local supervisory authority.
